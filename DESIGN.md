## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the warmth, sunlight, and artisanal hospitality of a high-end coastal trattoria set in Mallorca. It merges authentic Italian culinary heritage with relaxed Balearic sophistication. The visual philosophy departs from cold, generic restaurant templates, cultivating an inviting, tactile editorial atmosphere that evokes raw linen, sun-bleached stone, terracotta, and fresh garden ingredients.

The target audience encompasses international travelers, discerning local residents, and food enthusiasts seeking an authentic yet polished dining experience. The interface evokes generous hospitality, craft pride, sensory appetite, and seamless accessibility. The aesthetic blends **Warm Mediterranean Organic Modernism** with contemporary editorial craftsmanship, prioritizing generous negative space, gentle organic curves, and rich, appetizing accents.

## Colors
The palette captures the essence of sun-drenched coastal dining:

- **Primary (`#C0392B`)**: Deep Italian San Marzano Tomato red. Reserved for primary calls-to-action (e.g., table reservations, main ordering triggers, key highlights). For hover and pressed states, shift toward `#A93226`.
- **Secondary (`#2E5D3A`)**: Mediterranean Basil Green. Represents nature, garden-to-table provenance, dietary indicators, wine lists, and secondary navigation elements.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366`)**: WhatsApp Green. Exclusively reserved for instant guest support, concierge desk queries, and direct table reservations via WhatsApp. It must never be repurposed for generic system statuses or standard buttons.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1F2421`)**: Soft Charcoal with an olive undertone. Ensures high contrast legibility while avoiding the clinical harshness of pure black. Secondary copy uses `#5C615D`.
- **Surfaces & Canvases**: Base canvas uses warm off-white cream (`#FCFBF7`), layered against elevated card surfaces of pure alabaster (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted cream container surfaces (`#F8F6F0`). Thin separator lines utilize warm linen borders (`#EFECE4`).

## Typography
The typographic system pairs the artisanal, literary romance of **Playfair Display** with the clear, human-centric geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Playfair Display** commands the editorial narrative: section titles, culinary categories, hero claims, and evocative dish names. Use medium to semi-bold weights for headlines. The `label-caption` style leverages Playfair's italic cut for wine pairings, chef's tasting notes, and regional provenance subtitles.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides functional clarity: dish descriptions, prices, menu filters, interactive forms, reservation selectors, and transactional metadata. Its rounded, open letterforms mirror the organic softness of the brand while maintaining legibility under bright outdoor light conditions.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an unhurried, editorial rhythm that gives culinary photography and course menus room to breathe:

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column responsive grid with a maximum container width of `1280px`. Column gutters are `1.5rem` (`24px`), with outer canvas padding of `3rem` (`48px`). Menu pages feature an asymmetrical balance—a sticky side panel for course categories (Antipasti, Primi, Secondi, Dolci) paired with a 2-column dish showcase.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Multi-column cards shift to a comfortable 2-up grid.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer page margins. Reservation actions detach to a fixed bottom sticky bar to enable one-handed booking on the move.
- **Vertical Cadence**: Standard inter-section spacing expands to `space-xl` (`2.5rem` to `4.5rem`), creating an unhurried browsing pace reminiscent of a leisurely Mediterranean meal.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through warm, natural daylight rather than artificial dark drops. Surfaces avoid sterile grey shadows in favor of terracotta- and charcoal-tinted ambient diffusions:

- **Flat / Canvas Level (`z-0`)**: Base background (`#FCFBF7`). Un-elevated, grounding the visitor in warm parchment tones.
- **Subtle Organic Surface (`z-1`)**: Menu cards, review panels, and informational modules rest at `#FFFFFF` with a warm ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(192, 57, 43, 0.02)`. An imperceptible border of `1px solid rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.06)` preserves boundary definition over bright cream backgrounds.
- **Interactive Hover Level (`z-2`)**: Triggered upon card hover or interactive focus: `box-shadow: 0 12px 30px -4px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(192, 57, 43, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a gentle `-2px` vertical translation.
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Bars (`z-3`)**: Dropdowns, date-pickers, mobile reservation bars, and dialogue modals: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.14)`.
- **Glassmorphic Accents**: Translucent navigation bars on scroll utilize `background: rgba(252, 251, 247, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a soft border of `rgba(239, 236, 228, 0.8)`.

## Shapes
The shape language takes inspiration from handcrafted Italian ceramics, olive leaves, and smoothed Mediterranean pebbles. Sharp edges are eliminated in favor of warm, approachable curves.

- **Cards and Major Containers**: Built with `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem` / `20px`) and `rounded-3xl` (`1.75rem` / `28px`). Dish images within cards carry matching nested radii (`1rem` / `16px`).
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Filter Pills)**: Pill-shaped (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) for high-touch triggers (reservation CTAs, tags, floating buttons) to invite tactile contact.
- **Form Fields & Inputs**: Styled with generous `1rem` (`16px`) radii, providing a modern yet soft appearance.
- **Badges and Indicators**: Fully rounded pills with balanced internal horizontal padding.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservations, Bookings)**: Solid `#C0392B` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.875rem 2rem`. Subtle transition (`transform 0.2s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), background-color 0.2s ease`). Hover shifts to `#A93226` with slight scale (`1.015`).
- **Secondary Action (View Menu, Our Story)**: Outline variant with a `1.5px` border in `#2E5D3A`, text in `#2E5D3A`, transparent background. On hover: fills with `#2E5D3A`, text switches to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Exclusive WhatsApp Action**: Solid `#25D366` with `#FFFFFF` text, accompanied by the WhatsApp mark. Padded `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover deepens to `#1EBE5B`. Exclusively dedicated to direct messaging, group event inquiries, or concierge instant bookings.
- **Tertiary / Text Button**: Editorial link with `#1F2421`, styled with Playfair Display italic or bold Plus Jakarta Sans, featuring an animated underline accent in `#C0392B`.

### Menu Cards & Food Items
- Enclosed in an elevated card with `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-2xl` geometry, and subtle warm drop shadow.
- Dishes present the name in Playfair Display (`headline-sm`), price aligned right in Plus Jakarta Sans (`subhead-lg`, `#2E5D3A`), and ingredient narrative below in `#5C615D` (`body-md`).
- Tag dietary markers (e.g., *Vegetariano*, *Senza Glutine*, *Locale Mallorquín*) at the top edge using miniature rounded chips.

### Chips & Course Selectors
- Pill-shaped category filters (e.g., "Pasta Fresca", "Vinos de Mallorca", "Dolci").
- Unselected: Cream container `#F8F6F0`, border `#EFECE4`, text `#1F2421`.
- Active/Selected: Rich Basil green `#2E5D3A`, text `#FFFFFF`, without border, elevating slightly.

### Form Inputs & Date/Time Selectors
- Built for seamless mobile reservation booking.
- Background in `#FFFFFF` with border `#EFECE4`, text in `#1F2421`, `rounded-xl` (`1rem`).
- Height set to comfortable touch minimum (`48px` to `52px`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#C0392B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(192, 57, 43, 0.15)`).

### Dish Dietary & Provenance Badges
- Compact pill-shaped tags: Soft tinted backgrounds (e.g., `#2E5D3A` at 10% opacity with `#2E5D3A` text for organic Mallorca produce; `#C0392B` at 10% opacity for House Specialties).

### Modal & Bottom Sheet (Mobile Booking)
- Mobile reservation flows present a drawer sliding from the bottom with `rounded-t-3xl` corners, dragging handle in `#EFECE4`, and immediate access to guest count, date picker, and tomato-red confirmation CTA.
